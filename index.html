Common Questions.

What's the minimum commitment? +
We work on a monthly retainer with a 2-month minimum. This gives us enough time to build your creator pipeline and deliver consistent results before evaluating fit.
How fast can we get started? +
Most clients are fully onboarded within 5–7 business days of signing. Creator sourcing begins immediately and first deliverables typically land within 2–3 weeks.
How many revisions do we get? +
Every deliverable comes with one round of revisions included. Additional revision rounds can be arranged depending on your tier and brief complexity.
Do we own the content? +
Yes — all content produced through TrendSet Media is fully owned by your company. We handle creator licensing as part of the service.
Can we upgrade or change tiers? +
Absolutely. You can upgrade at any time and we'll adjust your pipeline accordingly. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.
What platforms do you create content for? +
We primarily produce content for TikTok, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), YouTube and Amazon. All content is formatted and optimized for the platform your client needs.
